# Bird sound references

Two recordings by **Oona Räisänen (Mysid)**, released as **Public domain** by the author on Wikimedia Commons. Metadata checked 2026-10-11 via the Commons API. The recordings describe the principal target species, not an assertion that no other sound exists in the clip.

- `blackbird.ogg`: [Turdus merula 2.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Turdus_merula_2.ogg), southern Finland, 2007-05-21. `blackbird.mp3` is the first 12 seconds, mechanically trimmed and converted to MP3 at 128 kbit/s.
- `sparrow.ogg`: [Passer domesticus.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Passer_domesticus.ogg), nesting house sparrows in suburban southern Finland, 2007-05-31. `sparrow.mp3` is the approximately 11-second recording converted to MP3 at 128 kbit/s.

No calls were synthesized or relabeled. The species drawings are original schematic SVG illustrations, not photographs or classification output. The user's separate `nature.mp3` ambience remains an unannotated listening track and is not used as the answer key.
